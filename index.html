How to read this page

FII, DII, Client, Pro
FII is foreign institutions, DII is domestic institutions (mutual funds, insurers), Client is retail and other traders, Pro is proprietary trading desks of brokers.
Long, Short, Net
Long means contracts bought, a bet on a rise. Short means contracts sold, a bet on a fall. Net is long minus short.
Index vs stock segments
Index futures and options track Nifty or Bank Nifty. Stock futures and options track single shares. This data is by category, not by stock name.
Open interest (OI)
Number of contracts still open. Rising OI with rising price usually means fresh buying; rising OI with falling price usually means fresh selling.
Put-call ratio (PCR)
Put OI divided by call OI. Above 1 is often read as bullish support, below 0.7 as bearish.
Max pain
The strike where option buyers collectively lose the most. Prices often drift toward it near expiry.
SMA and EMA
Moving averages of closing price over N days. SMA is the plain average; EMA weights recent days more. Price above them suggests an uptrend.
Support and resistance
Support is a price zone where falls have repeatedly stopped. Resistance is a zone where rises have repeatedly stalled. Zones here come from swing highs and lows over the last year.
Fibonacci levels
Retracements show how deep a pullback might go inside the last rally (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%). Extensions project how far the move could stretch beyond the last high (127.2%, 161.8%).

Analysis

Dollar Index fell 0.09%. A stronger dollar usually weighs on the rupee and on FII flows into emerging markets like India; a weaker dollar is generally supportive.

Brent crude rose 0.64%. India imports most of its oil, so higher crude widens the import bill, pressures the rupee and inflation, and is a headwind for the market; falling crude is a tailwind.

India VIX rose 7.51%. Rising VIX means options are pricing bigger moves, which makes option buying costlier; falling VIX favours option writers.

USD/INR fell 0.67%. A weaker rupee tends to go with FII selling; a firmer rupee with inflows.
